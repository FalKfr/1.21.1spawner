SPAWNER PROTECT (Fabric client mod, Minecraft 1.21.11)

Needs a SILK TOUCH PICKAXE in your hotbar. Without one it does nothing (and warns you in chat).
When another player is rendered near you and a skeleton spawner is in range, it holds shift,
breaks every skeleton spawner with the silk touch pickaxe, then logs you off.
Press P to turn it ON/OFF (status shows top-left).

INSTALL: Fabric Loader 0.18+, Fabric API for 1.21.11, Java 21. Put the jar in .minecraft/mods.
